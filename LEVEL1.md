## How the dispatcher chose orders

At level 1 each accepted request earns the list price of its first option. The dispatcher can decline a request by leaving it unquoted. It checks the road time and the latest feasible pickup against both sites’ opening hours. For cars it wins, it posts a one-car gig and adjusts its fee while it remains open.

**Policy 1.** The filters included single-car orders, drives up to 250 minutes, at least 75 minutes from the quote deadline to the latest feasible pickup, at least 240 minutes for drives over 120 minutes. The base opening fee was 69.00% of the typical fee before adjustments.
